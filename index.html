Do I need to choose a service first?+

No. Start with a conversation about your goals. Angee can explain the available options and the clinical review needed before a visit.

Can you come to my location?+

Texas Drips is a mobile service. Call with your location to confirm the current service area, travel arrangements, and availability.

How much does a visit cost?+

Ask for current pricing when you get in touch. Confirm the chosen service, any add-ons, and travel charges before your appointment.

Is every service right for everyone?+

No. Eligibility is assessed individually. Discuss your health history, medications, and questions with the clinical team before proceeding.
